# Positioning

Positioning comes before copy. Use with [research](../research.md) and [copy](../copy.md). A full repositioning needs its own skill and real buyer interviews.

## Five questions first

1. What would the buyer do if you did not exist? Include doing nothing, a cheap DIY fix, and the old way.
2. What do you have that those do not?
3. So what? What does that let the buyer do?
4. Who cares the most about that?
5. What kind of thing should they think you are, so your value is obvious?

Write each answer with its source. A guess stays **PROPOSED**.

## Plays to test

| Play | Why it can work |
|---|---|
| Compete with "do nothing" | Many lost sales go to staying put, not to a rival |
| Name the hidden cause | Saying why past fixes failed makes your fix believable |
| Make the mechanism the hero, not the company | Buyers trust a clear "how" more than brand talk |
| Pick a lane on price | Clearly cheapest or clearly premium is easier to grasp than the middle |
| Narrow who it is for | A tight group sees itself in the words and proof |
| Theme for cold buyers, details for warm ones | Cold buyers buy a story; warm buyers want the how |
| In B2B, arm the inside champion | One person sells it to the rest of their team |

## Watch outs

Do not invent a mechanism. It must be true for the product and approved where claims matter. Re-check the page, ads, and onboarding after a positioning change so they all say the same thing.
