# Keeping buyers

Use with [After purchase](../post-purchase.md). Messaging needs consent, a sender, an owner, and stop rules. A draft does not allow sending.

## Why it matters

A renewal or a win back usually costs far less than a new buyer. No ad spend, often less sales cost. Teams that wait to build this often say they left a lot of money behind.

## Plays to test

| Play | Why it can work | What to watch |
|---|---|---|
| Start the renewal talk well before the end date | Gives time to show results and plan year two | Renewal rate |
| Frame renewal as the next stage, not the same thing again | People renew into a new goal | Renewal rate, upgrade rate |
| A lower cost "stay in" plan, like group help | Keeps people who would leave, at lower delivery cost | Downsell take rate, churn |
| Long, useful email to engaged readers | Content that helps keeps the brand in mind; offers live inside it | Clicks, buys, unsubscribes |
| Win back past buyers when new sales slow | Past buyers already trust you | Win back buys |
| Match payment plan length to program length | Paying after the help ends feels bad | Failed payments, churn |
| Make the cheap product about fast wins | Buyers who win early buy again | Repeat and move up rate |

## Watch outs

- Most churn comes from a mismatch: wrong plan terms, no next goal, or a product that does not solve the next problem. Fix the cause before adding messages.
- Two flows must not send conflicting asks. See the recovery table in [After purchase](../post-purchase.md).
