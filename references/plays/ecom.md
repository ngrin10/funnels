# Online stores

Use with [pages](../surfaces/pages.md) and [forms](../surfaces/forms.md). Pricing and margin choices need a finance skill and the real costs.

## Know the math first

Work out profit per product after shipping, fees, discounts, and returns. Know how much ad spend each sale can carry. Then do page work.

## Plays to test

| Play | Why it can work | What to watch |
|---|---|---|
| Subscribe as the default choice | Repeat orders pay for a thin first order | Subscribe rate, cancels in first months |
| Gift with order instead of percent off | Keeps the price strong and still gives a reason to buy now | Order rate, average order, repeat rate |
| Fewer bundle choices | Too many choices slows people down | Add to cart rate |
| Rebuild the top of the product page | Most people never scroll far | Add to cart rate |
| Real reviews and photos near the button | Proof where the doubt happens | Add to cart, returns |
| Landing page that matches the ad angle | A home page cannot match every ad | Conversion by ad group |
| Find what a big rival does badly and win only that | A clear gap is easier to own than "better at all of it" | Share of searches, reviews |
| Welcome popup plus a strong welcome email set | Most new list growth comes from the first visit | List growth, first order rate |

## Watch outs

- Do not fake low stock or a timer. It breaks trust and can break rules.
- "Brand" comes from selling lots of product to a clear group. A new store should sell first, polish later.
- Judge store tests on profit per visitor or orders, not add to cart alone.
