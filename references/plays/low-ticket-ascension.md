# Low ticket to high ticket

A low ticket product is a cheap first sale. Its main job is to find real buyers fast and move some of them to a bigger offer. It is rarely the profit by itself.

Read [Offers](offers.md) and [Call funnels](call-funnels.md) too.

## The usual path

Ad, then a short sales page, then a two step checkout (contact first, card second), then an optional bump, then a page that books a short call, then a call with a setter or closer, then the bigger offer.

## Plays to test

| Play | Why it can work | What to watch |
|---|---|---|
| Make the cheap product a small slice of the big one | The buyer gets a taste of the same help, so the next step feels natural | Rate of buyers who move up |
| Send buyers straight to booking after payment | Every extra page between payment and booking loses people | Booked calls per buyer |
| Name the call for what it does for them ("setup call", "plan call") | People show up for help they already bought into | Book rate and show rate |
| Build for a fast first win, not a long course | Most buyers never finish a course. The ones who win early move up | Use of the product in the first days |
| Two step checkout | Shows where people leave, and lets you follow up with the ones who left | Checkout finish rate, recovered sales |
| Order bump that saves time | A tool or shortcut gets taken more than "more lessons" | Bump rate, and booking rate after |
| Keep the first product broad | A very narrow product caps how many buyers you can get each day | Daily buyers at the same cost |

## The numbers that matter

Two numbers decide if this works: cost to get a buyer, after the cheap sale pays some of it back, and the share of buyers who move up. A fancy front end with poor move-up is a losing model.

Public talks report rough ranges, such as about half or more of buyers booking a call and a few percent moving to high ticket. These are **STATED** by other teams. Use them only to sense check. Use this project's own data for goals.

## Watch outs

- Upsell pages between payment and booking can raise first order value and lower move-up. Test the whole path, not one page.
- Low ticket buyers can flood a small sales team. Check team capacity before you scale.
- Keep refund and access paths clear. A cheap sale still needs real delivery. See [After purchase](../post-purchase.md).
