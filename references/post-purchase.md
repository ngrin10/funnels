# After purchase, calls, and later use

The flow does not end at payment. Map delivery and first value before adding another sale.

## After purchase

Confirm what was bought, show access, state what happens next, and explain how to get help. Handle a missing receipt, failed login, delayed delivery, wrong email, duplicate purchase, and refund path as needed.

An optional next offer must not make the first purchase feel incomplete or hide access. State what extra help it gives, who it fits, the full terms, and the way to decline. Check fit with the offer brief. Get specialist help before choosing a new offer or money plan.

## Booking and confirmation

State the purpose of the call honestly. Keep its name and promise the same across the page, calendar, messages, and team. Define who can book, available capacity, timezone, routing, cancel, reschedule, and no-slot behavior.

After booking, show the time, channel, join link, person or team, useful prep, and contact route. Answer real pre-call questions with supported evidence. Keep optional reading separate from access to the appointment.

Do not copy a fixed reminder stack. Choose channels and timing from the project's consent rules, customer needs, and team capacity. Assign a sender and owner. Explain stop rules for cancel, reschedule, show, and unsubscribe. A message draft does not authorize sending.

## Recovery and later use

| State | Useful next job | End condition |
|---|---|---|
| Bought, cannot get access | Help them reach what they bought | Access works or support owns the case |
| Started booking, no appointment | Offer a clear route or help if allowed | Booked, declined, or opted out |
| Booked, needs prep | Provide the facts needed to attend | Call happens, changes, or is cancelled |
| Missed call | Offer an allowed way to reschedule | Rebooked, declined, or no more contact |
| Setup stalled | Help with the actual blocked step | First value, help case, or stop rule |
| Payment failed | Explain the issue and safe repair | Paid, cancelled, or owner-defined end |
| Renewal or return | Explain the real terms and next useful action | Renewed, left, or opted out |

For each used row, write trigger, group, channel, delay, sender, exact copy, allowed data, exclusions, repeat limit, exit, owner, and events. Prevent two flows from sending conflicting asks.

## Read results with limits

Keep bookings, held calls, qualified calls, paid sales, delivery, and later value separate. Use a stated outcome window and mature groups. Check capacity and delivery work before proposing more volume. A high booking rate can still produce poor fit or overload the team.

Do not claim that messages caused the result from a before-and-after read alone. Use the limits check when the owner needs that answer.
