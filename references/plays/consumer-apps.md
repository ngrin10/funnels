# Consumer apps

Use with [onboarding](../surfaces/onboarding.md) and [paywalls](../surfaces/paywalls.md). App store rules, billing, and refunds need the right owner.

## Three checks before you buy growth

1. Do enough viewers download?
2. Do most people who open the app reach the paywall?
3. Do enough people who see the paywall pay?

Fix the weakest one first. Spending on ads before these work just buys more leaks. Public talks give rough targets for each. Treat those as **STATED** and set this project's own goals.

## Plays to test

| Play | Why it can work | What to watch |
|---|---|---|
| Build the paywall first, then the onboarding that leads to it | The paywall is how the app gets paid | Paywall view rate, pay rate |
| Sell the dream result, not the feature | People pay for the result in their life | Pay rate, refunds |
| Longer onboarding for a painful problem | Each step can restate the pain and the fix | Paywall reach, pay rate |
| Short onboarding for a simple tool | People want to start fast | Time to first use |
| Ask for payment before use | A free tier can hide weak messaging | Pay rate, early cancels |
| Search ads on rival app names | Many installs start with a search | Cost per payer |

## Watch outs

- Plan for the store's cut and payout timing in the money math.
- A hard paywall must state the trial, price, renewal, and cancel steps clearly. See [paywalls](../surfaces/paywalls.md).
- Count payers who stay, not trial starts.
