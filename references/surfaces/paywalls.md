# Paywalls, upgrades, and checkout

Read the exact plan and billing state. A public pricing page and an in-product upgrade ask have different jobs.

## Know the moment

Is the person hitting a feature limit, ending a trial, choosing a plan, upgrading, or recovering a failed payment? What have they already received? What are they trying to do now? Do not use the same screen rules for every state.

## Check terms for each path

| State or plan | Amount due now | Later amount and date | Trial or renewal | Included access | Fees / taxes / credits | Cancel and refund rules | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Read values from the source that owns them. Define currency, period, rounding, and missing-data behavior. Check the math. Avoid typed prices, limits, or dates that can change in config. The wording must stay true for every selectable plan.

## Help the person choose

Show the task or benefit the paid option enables. Keep comparisons fair and mark the current plan. Explain what happens if they decline or the trial ends. Do not claim free access continues unless it does.

Give the main action a clear label, including commitment where needed. Keep a valid way back. If another message would interrupt a payment repair or cancellation, define which takes priority. Do not use stacked offers to hide the original task.

## Define payment and access states

Cover submit, pending, success, decline, retry, duplicate action, partial success, and delayed access. Say what confirms payment and what grants access. A clicked button is not a paid order. A payment event alone may not prove that the correct access was granted.

For plan changes, state when the change starts, what happens to past data, and how billing is handled. These decisions require the project's actual rules. Get the right billing skill or owner when they are unknown.

## Prove it

Use sandbox payments for each affected path. Check the amount, terms, receipt, access, tracking, failure, and recovery. Do not charge a real card without authorization.

Measure paid outcomes and value at a named horizon, plus refunds, errors, and retention where needed. Do not pick a winner on plan clicks alone. New pricing strategy, forecasts, and test conclusions need the right skills and data.
