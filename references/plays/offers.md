# Offers and money plans

Use with [the offer brief](../offer-brief.md). A new price or money plan still needs an offer or finance skill and checked costs.

## The shape most strong offers share

- **First offer pays for the ad.** The first thing a new buyer buys tries to cover what it cost to get them. Profit comes later, from the next offers.
- **Next offers raise profit.** An upsell sells more of the same help, or the next step, or a faster way. It is offered when the buyer feels the next problem, not when they feel done.
- **A downsell saves the no.** If they say no, change the terms (pay over time, a smaller version, fewer features). Do not just cut the price of the same thing. That teaches people to wait for a discount.
- **Ongoing offers steady the cash.** A plan that renews (membership, refills, group help) turns one sale into many.

Not every business needs all four parts. Check which ones the project has today before adding one.

## Plays to test

| Play | When it fits | What to watch |
|---|---|---|
| Sell the next problem | Buyer just got the first result and has a new, clear need | Take rate, refunds, first-offer satisfaction |
| Offer "A or B", not "yes or no" | There are two real choices that both help | Choice split, regret, refunds |
| Unsell what they do not need | Trust is the main block | Average order, refunds, reviews |
| Order bump of a tool or shortcut | Cheap first product; bump makes the main thing faster | Bump take rate, and check the next step did not drop |
| Payment plan as the downsell | Price is the stated reason for no | Collected cash, failed payments, length of plan vs length of program |
| Make the guarantee about an action | Risk is the main fear; an outcome promise is not allowed or not safe | Refund rate, claims, approval from the right person |
| Fix risk before adding bonuses | Bonuses keep growing but sales do not | Sale rate before and after |

## Watch outs

- Count gross profit, not revenue, when you judge if an offer pays.
- A payment plan longer than the program it pays for tends to cause failed payments and churn.
- More bonuses do not fix an offer people do not trust.
- A scarcity line must be true. Fake deadlines cost trust and can break rules.
