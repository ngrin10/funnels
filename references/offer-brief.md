# Offer brief

Use this when the idea changes what is sold, how it is sold, or what comes next. Skip it with a reason for a small UI repair.

This is a map of known terms and open choices. It is not a pricing or finance model. Use the limits check before making those decisions.

## Fill these fields

| Field | What to write |
|---|---|
| Buyer and need | Who this helps and the job they want done |
| Offer | What is included, what is not, and what the buyer gets |
| Delivery | Who supplies it, how they get access, and when |
| Price and terms | Currency, amount, tax treatment, billing period, trial, renewal, refund and cancel rules, with the source |
| Main next step | Buy, try, book, apply, or use the product; name the real destination |
| First value | What useful thing happens after purchase |
| Next offer, if any | What extra need it serves and why the first purchase still stands alone |
| Limits | Fit, access, capacity, promises that cannot be made |
| Proof | Checked claims and permission to use them |
| Business goal | What the owner wants to improve, and at what time horizon |
| Cost and cash inputs | What is known, missing, or reported by someone else |
| Owner and open choices | Who settles each gap and what it changes |

## Check whether the path fits

Does the entry offer attract the person the product can help? Does each later offer add a clear form of help? Does one tier promise something another tier requires? Is the same call or service named the same way across pages and messages?

Write the flow one step per line. Say what the buyer receives and what choice comes next. Keep optional purchases separate from access to what was already bought.

If the owner wants to improve order value, later sales, retention, or payback, record that goal. These goals can pull in different directions. Ask who decides the tradeoff. Do not choose a money plan from a few page metrics.

## Read the full terms

Check every plan and path. Fine print for one plan must not show for all plans. Discounts must use the right base and period. A trial is not the same as a refund window. A selected plan is not a paid plan. A later promise must match the earlier page, checkout, receipt, and product.

Show unresolved terms to the owner before dependent copy or payment work. Get a suitable specialist skill for a new pricing decision or a forecast. Keep its inputs, assumptions, and limits visible.
