# Sales videos and webinars

A sales video often does most of the selling in a call funnel or a buy-now funnel. Treat it like your best ad: test it often, not once a year.

## Plays to test

| Play | When it fits | What to watch |
|---|---|---|
| Hide the button until part of the video plays | Cold, curious visitors who need to learn first | Buyers or held calls, not clicks |
| Show the button right away | Warm visitors who already want it (from content, referrals, retargeting) | Same; a delay can lose ready buyers |
| Open with the mechanism (why their past tries failed) | The market has heard every promise already | Watch time, next step rate |
| Blame the system, not the buyer | Buyers feel shame about the problem (money, weight, debt) | Watch time, comments, refunds |
| Fix the guarantee first | Lots of watching, few buyers | Sale rate |
| Make several versions with one change each | One video has stalled | Cost per buyer for each |
| Turn a winning video into a cheap class | The hooks already work; some people want a smaller first step | New buyers who did not buy before |

## Length and format

There is no right length. Shorter in-feed videos and longer page videos both work for different buyers. Pick from this project's tests and buyer's level of interest, not from a rule you heard.

## Watch outs

- Judge a video on buyers or held calls, never on views or opt-ins alone.
- One video, one face, one funnel is a risk. When it stops working, it all stops. Plan a second path before you need it.
- Every claim in the video must be true and approved. See [copy](../copy.md).
