# Read numbers with care

This guide helps name and check the data needed for a spec. It does not teach every cause of a result or replace an analytics, test, or field-specific skill.

## Define the number

Record the tool and account, pull date, time window, people included/excluded, what is counted, units, and the base of a rate. Say whether the number was checked in a system, reported by someone, calculated from inputs, or not known.

Keep a click, selected plan, paid order, held call, collected cash, delivered value, and later use separate. Do not treat one as another. Check fees, refunds, failed payments, and delayed results when they affect the question.

## Check the read

Is the account right? Does the event fire? Did its meaning change? Are duplicate records handled? Are both reads from the same time and group rules? Have people had time to finish? Is this a sample, a full group, or unknown?

Keep conflicting reads with source dates until settled. A missing event is not a real zero. A newer export can still use the wrong rules.

## Know what it cannot prove

Many inputs can move one number. A page change may overlap with a change in traffic, offer, tracking, device mix, timing, or other work. These are checks to consider, not a complete model or a diagnosis.

When asked why, say what was observed, what is not ruled out, and what evidence and skill are needed. Do not infer cause from a before-and-after result, a correlation, or a tidy story. Ask the few missing questions that would change the answer. Do not dump a long list of variables on the user.

## Rank with honest limits

A priority estimate may use people reaching the step, an expected absolute change, and value per result at a stated horizon. Use only a supplied estimate or a model the user asked for. Do not invent an expected lift. If it is missing, leave the rank open. Label the estimate as a guess and the total as MODELED. Show effort, confidence, costs, and missing inputs. Do not call this a forecast or causal result.

For a new money model, forecast, or acquisition decision, get the relevant specialist skill and checked inputs. A spec can record the needed model without inventing it.

## Plan a test with the right help

Name the hypothesis, control, variant, who is assigned, stable assignment rule, split, exclusions, exposure, main result, guardrails, outcome delay, sample plan, minimum duration, stop rule, and read owner/date.

The sample and stop method depend on the baseline, minimum useful change, traffic, and chosen statistical method. If these or the right skill are missing, mark the test plan incomplete. Do not call a winner at a fixed count or impose a universal holdout or duration.

Define immediate failure stops separately from success calls. A broken payment flow may need to stop at once. Noise in the result is not a reason to invent an early win. State uncertainty and the limits of the test when reading it.

For a confirmed repair, document rollout and recovery without forcing a test. Keep a measurement gap separate from whether an unrelated repair can be built.
