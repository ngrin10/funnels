# Call funnels and show rate

Use with [After purchase](../post-purchase.md). Booking rules, consent, and messages are this project's choice. Do not copy a reminder stack.

## The big idea

Whether people show up is mostly decided before the call. The ad, page, booking screen, and confirmation page set what they expect. If the call feels like the next step in something they already want, more show up.

## Plays to test

| Play | Why it can work | What to watch |
|---|---|---|
| One name for the call, everywhere | "Free consult" on the ad and "strategy session" on the calendar feels like a switch | Show rate, close rate |
| Count cost per held call, not per booked call | It puts show rate on the marketing side of the work | Held calls per dollar |
| Keep the booking window short | Long gaps let interest fade | Show rate by days to call |
| A real person confirms the call | A human check tends to beat automation alone | Show rate, team time per show |
| Confirmation page with short answer videos | Answers the top doubts before the call, in the same voice as the ad | Show rate, call length, close rate |
| Repeat the "why this can work for you" from the sales video | Restating belief beats bringing up new doubts like price after booking | Show rate, close rate |
| Show helpful content to people who booked | Keeps the brand in view until the call | Show rate, cost |
| Give away useful info before a B2B call | Prepared buyers feel less nervous and show up | Show rate, sales cycle length |

## Read results with care

Keep booked, held, qualified, and closed apart. A better book rate with worse fit is not a win. More shows that swamp the team is not a win either. Check capacity first.

Close rate can move money faster than any ad change. If close rate is weak, name that as its own problem and get a sales skill or owner.
