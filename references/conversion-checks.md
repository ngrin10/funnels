# Conversion checks by surface

Use only the relevant checks. Treat each change as a hypothesis unless a broken behavior is confirmed. No pattern guarantees lift.

| Surface | Ask | Check | Judge beyond the tap |
|---|---|---|---|
| Landing page | Who arrives, from which promise, to do what? | Match the entry message; make the offer and main action clear; show supported proof and answer real objections; inspect phone layout and destination. | Qualified leads, paid buyers, or value per eligible visitor |
| Signup | Which fields are needed before value? What happens after submit? | Explain required data; use visible labels, autofill and useful errors; preserve input; cover verification, duplicate account, auth failure and return paths. | Valid accounts reaching first value |
| Form | Who uses each answer and what happens next? | Justify each field; distinguish required/optional; use one clear topic at a time; test validation, failed submit, saved progress and confirmation. | Useful submissions and downstream quality |
| Popup | What triggers it, who sees it, and how often? | Define timing, dismissal, cooldown, suppression after success, mobile space, keyboard focus, escape and return focus. Keep the decline clear. | Incremental outcomes, dismissals and lost page completions |
| Paywall / checkout | What value was shown? What plan, price, currency, tax, renewal and entitlement apply? | Read each plan and payment state; use live config; explain billing; cover success, decline, retry, duplicate charge prevention, upgrade and cancellation route. | Collected value, refunds, retention and payment errors |
| Onboarding | What real action gives first value? What blocks it? | Focus on the next useful action; make empty states useful; support skip/resume; separate setup completion from actual value; handle stalled and returning people. | Activation, time to value and later retention |
| Next offer | What has the buyer received and what problem comes next? | Keep the first purchase useful alone; clarify optional extras, delivery, terms, support and total cost; do not block access behind another sale. | Net value at a stated horizon, refunds and fulfillment load |
| Booking / sales | Who qualifies, who follows up, and is there capacity? | Cover timezone, availability, qualification, confirmation, reminders, cancel/reschedule and handoff ownership. | Qualified held calls, sales and capacity, not bookings alone |
| Lifecycle | What behavior triggers and ends each message? | Specify consent, channel, delay, suppression, timezone, unsubscribe and conflicting sequences. Use templates with missing-data fallbacks. | Relevant next action and unsubscribes/complaints |

For any added step, state what it replaces or why the extra effort is worth testing. Do not use fixed field-count penalties, conversion benchmarks, ad cutoffs, or test lengths as facts about this product.
