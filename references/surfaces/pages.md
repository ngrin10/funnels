# Pages

Use for landing, home, pricing, feature, and content pages. Use the signup or form guide for the action inside the page.

## Ask what changes the work

What kind of page is this? Who arrives and what were they promised? What should they do next? What counts as a useful result? Reuse answers from the context doc.

Read the whole page on the relevant devices and follow the next step. Record the version and what was checked. A screenshot cannot prove that the button, form, or payment works.

## Read in order

1. Can the reader tell what the offer is, who it helps, and what to do?
2. Does the main message match the entry source and the next screen?
3. Does the image show the product, result, or process the text explains?
4. Does the proof support the nearby claim, with permission to use it?
5. Are real doubts answered before the choice where they matter?
6. Are terms, prices, form effort, page speed, and mobile layout clear enough to act?

Fix a confirmed broken link or false term as a repair. Treat a proposed headline, content order, or layout as a test idea unless there is stronger evidence. A familiar design pattern is not proof of lift.

## Match the page type

- Home: help new readers understand the product and reach the right path. Buyers with different needs may need clear routes.
- Landing: carry the entry promise through to one main goal. Keep useful help; do not remove navigation blindly.
- Pricing: help the buyer compare real plans, limits, and billing periods. Mark the current plan and explain any recommendation.
- Feature: show the task it helps with, how it works, limits, and a next step.
- Content: place a useful next step where it fits the reader's task. Do not turn every paragraph into a sales ask.

## Deliver

For each finding, show the screen or source, the issue, who it affects, the proposed change, its reason, what is still a guess, and the success measure. Separate confirmed repairs from test ideas. Give exact copy options only for the parts being changed.

Check the destination, long text, phone layout, keyboard path, and loading or failed actions. Judge the downstream result too. More clicks can still bring fewer useful buyers.
